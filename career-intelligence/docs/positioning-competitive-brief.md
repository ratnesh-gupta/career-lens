**CareerLens doesn’t sell resume templates — it scores how the job market sees your career, then shows what to fix next.**

## 2. Category

| Not this | This |
|----------|------|
| Resume builder / template marketplace | **Personal career intelligence** |
| “Make my PDF pretty” | **“How does the job market see me?”** |

**Primary job-to-be-done:** Understand market-readiness and close the gap — not pick a layout.

---

## 3. Positioning statement

For **professionals who already have a resume** and need a clear signal of competitiveness,  
**CareerLens** is a **career intelligence platform** that turns resume evidence into a **versioned Career Score**, breakdown, and shareable proof of market-readiness —  
unlike **resume editors and template tools**, which optimize document design, not how the market reads the career.

---

## 4. Product pillars (what’s different)

1. **Career Profile is canonical** — identity lives on the profile; the resume is *evidence*, not the product.
2. **Career Score is the core output** — overall score, grade, percentile, strengths, weaknesses, recommendations.
3. **Deterministic, versioned scoring** — weights are product config (e.g. `r1a-1.0`); AI extracts and analyzes, it does not invent the final number.
4. **Viral loop, not file download** — shareable score card + public profile (R1a), not “export PDF and leave.”
5. **Roadmap stays on intelligence** — R1b: target role, JD fit, guided optimization — still market signal, not stationery.

---

## 5. Competitive map (simplified)

### Cluster A — Resume builders & templates
**Examples:** Novoresume, Kickresume, VisualCV, Canva resumes, many “AI resume” SaaS.

| They win on | CareerLens response |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Templates, design, export | We are not competing on themes |
| Speed to a pretty PDF | We compete on *clarity of market signal* |
| Upsell premium layouts | We upsell *role alignment & optimization* (R1b) |

### Cluster B — “ATS score” on a document
**Examples:** Tools that paste a resume and return an opaque 0–100.

| They win on | CareerLens response |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Instant single-file score | Same entry, but score is **profile-owned**, explainable, **versioned** |
| Keyword match theater | Categories + recommendations tied to career evidence |

### Cluster C — Career platforms / job-search suites
**Examples:** Teal, Huntr, some LinkedIn-adjacent workflows.

| They win on | CareerLens response |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Job tracker, CRM, broad toolkit | We stay narrow: **score → share → improve** |
| Breadth | We own **depth of the readiness signal** |

**Rule:** If the user’s only need is a template, they should use Cluster A. CareerLens is for users who need an answer to *how competitive am I?*

## 6. Messaging do’s and don’ts

**Do say**
- How does the job market see you?
- Career Score, market-readiness, strengths and gaps
- Share your score; improve what the market sees
- Profile-based, evidence from your resume

**Don’t say**
- “Best resume templates”
- “1,000 designs”
- “Beat ATS with keywords” as the whole story
- Positioning that makes us sound like another Canva-for-CVs

## 8. Objection handling

| Objection | Reply |
|-----------|--------|
| “There are already resume builders.” | Yes — we don’t build templates. We score market-readiness. |
| “Isn’t this just an ATS checker?” | ATS is one lens. Career Score is broader, explainable, and versioned. |
| “Why free score?” | R1a viral core: score → share → trust → later optimization (R1b). |
| “Can I edit my resume here?” | R1a is intelligence-first; guided optimization is R1b on top of score + target role. |

## 9. Success metrics (product/GTM alignment)

- % users who complete **Career Score** (not just upload)
- **Share rate** of public score / profile
- Return visits to improve score
- R1b conversion: target role → optimize (when live)
